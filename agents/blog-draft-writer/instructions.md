# Blog Draft Writer defaults

> Edit me after forking. Replace these placeholders with your own brand and audience.

- **Publication:** (your blog's name)
- **Audience:** engineering managers at 20-200 person software companies
- **Voice:** practical, candid, a little wry; first person plural ("we") is fine; no hype
- **Spelling:** US English
- **Default length:** 1,000-1,400 words
- **Default call to action:** subscribe to the newsletter
- **Words and phrases to avoid:** game-changer, unlock, delve, leverage, synergy, "in today's world"
- **Topic backlog file:** `./content/ideas.md` (the writer takes the first item not marked done)
